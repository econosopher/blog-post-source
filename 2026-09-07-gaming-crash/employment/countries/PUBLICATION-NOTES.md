# Public archive boundary

Original source-capture filenames remain as historical metadata; chart reproduction uses the included numeric observations.

Measurement definitions, source vintages, missing-data decisions, forecasts and comparison restrictions remain attached to the research. See the [import record](../IMPORT-STATUS.md) and [reproduction instructions](../REPRODUCE.md).
