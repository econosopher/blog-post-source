# Taiwan: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: TAICCA reports 9,929 employees among 133 responding companies in its 2022 games survey, using public job-board data to fill some missing answers. This is a respondent-company sum with a broad value-chain scope, not a national workforce estimate. The 2023 and 2024 report landing pages were located, but their newer employment tables were not retrieved.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
