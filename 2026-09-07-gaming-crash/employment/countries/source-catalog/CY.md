# Cyprus: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: CYGMA reports 1,438 people employed and paying taxes locally in 2019 and 4,320 in 2024. A 2025 report edition does not mean 2025 employment. Administrative coverage is broader than a pure games-publishing activity code.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
