# Indonesia: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: An official 2024 ecosystem study published in October 2025 reports at least 2,112 talents at game developers/publishers. Its public author abstract does not establish a complete national census or precise headcount date. Wider estimates of hundreds of thousands of affected jobs are economic impact, not core employment.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
