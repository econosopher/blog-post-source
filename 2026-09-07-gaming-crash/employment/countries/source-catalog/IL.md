# Israel: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: Deloitte/GameIS reports about 4,000 workers in 2017 and 14,000 in 2021 across a broad ecosystem. Domestic-only employment is not established, and the company roster includes global offices and real-money gaming. These are context estimates, excluded from the domestic comparison.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
