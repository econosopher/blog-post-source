# United Kingdom: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: TIGA provides production-role FTE snapshots from 2016 to May 2024 and workforce counts including freelancers through September 2025. DCMS supplies a separate 2015-2025 annual jobs series, subject to substantial sampling uncertainty. Ukie surveys provide contractor and nationality shares; changing recruitment prevents treating share changes as national workforce changes.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
