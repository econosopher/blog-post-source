# United States: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: Periodic ESA firm-mapping estimates cover 2015, 2016, 2019, 2023 and 2025. Core development/publishing can be derived from explicit subsectors for 2019/2023/2025. Broad totals are not a consistent time series; no verified national contractor or foreign-national share was found.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
