# Australia: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: Two distinct measures are available: ABS domestic business employment (headcount) and IGEA full-time employment including FTEs and contractors. IGEA added nonresponding-studio estimates for FY2025; do not interpret that change as a consistent survey trend.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
