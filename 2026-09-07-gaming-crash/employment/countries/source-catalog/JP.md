# Japan: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: CESA estimates 58,000–83,000 workers in core game companies and about 200,000 including related industries, but the public 2025 release does not date its employment observation. It also includes console hardware in core. CEDEC provides sample-only worker-status composition for 2024 and 2025.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
