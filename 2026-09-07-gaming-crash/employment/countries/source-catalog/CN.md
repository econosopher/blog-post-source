# China: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: CNG reports a selected major-listed-company personnel panel falling from 223,000 in 2021 to 205,800 in 2024. This is not verified as domestic game-only employment. The widely quoted 2018 national figure has undefined boundaries and cannot be joined to the panel.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
