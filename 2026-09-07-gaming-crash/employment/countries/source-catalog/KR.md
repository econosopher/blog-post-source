# South Korea: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: KOCCA reports 54,285 development/publishing workers in 2024, separately from 33,291 PC-cafe and arcade-venue workers. Verified annual core observations span 2015–2024. Repeated vintages show a one-person 2017 revision, retained explicitly.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
