# Singapore: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: A government parliamentary answer reports nearly 2,000 games-sector employees in 2021. The text places esports within games, and does not isolate a developer/publisher-only denominator. The previously quoted 2,700 is expected manpower demand, explicitly not an achieved employment count.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
