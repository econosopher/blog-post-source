# Netherlands: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: Games Monitor gives 3, 834 jobs in 2018, 4, 560 in 2021 and 4, 291 in 2023. Most recent decline reflects companies moving outside the games definition. Includes self-employed specialists and applied games.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
