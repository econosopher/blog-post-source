# Brazil: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: Abragames provides developer-workforce estimates for 2018, 2022 and 2023, including partners and workers at formal and informal developers. Outsourcing and foreign-worker figures exist only for changing employer samples. No newer verified national workforce observation was found by cutoff; the 2024 white paper repeats the 2023 estimate.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
