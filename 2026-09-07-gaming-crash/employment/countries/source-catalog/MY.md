# Malaysia: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: DOSM occupation categories combine digital games with esports, and the inspected MDEC Southeast Asia studies report studio/respondent samples and salary distributions. No defensible national domestic development/publishing employment stock was verified; regional sample sizes are not country workforce totals.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
