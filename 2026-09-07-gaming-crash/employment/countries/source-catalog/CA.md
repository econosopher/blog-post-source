# Canada: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: Annual Statistics Canada administrative ILUs cover 2015-2022, separate from periodic ESAC FTE estimates. The latest ESAC comparison is 35,250 revised FTEs in 2020-21 versus 34,010 in 2023-24. Contractor shares and 2015 immigration-status shares are survey-based, not comparable national citizenship counts.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
