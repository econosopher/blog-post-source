# Turkey: source catalog

Original 9 September assessment: TOGED supplies a 2023 employment estimate of 10,777 in the EGDF report. The questionnaire requests FTEs including remote workers abroad, so this is not strictly domestic workplace employment. A much lower 2,500-3,000 secondary estimate cannot be verified in its cited original and remains unplotted context.

Coverage ranges below include contextual and non-comparable series. A year span does not establish annual continuity.
