# Country employment: regional overview

[Current chart](country-employment-region-wrap.png) · [Editable SVG](country-employment-region-wrap.svg) · [Country inventory](regional-country-inventory.md) · [Source observations](region-wrap-selected-observations.csv)

The current chart includes **159 observations across 17 countries**, with 142 connections. Each region has an independent logarithmic employment range; all panels share the calendar axis and bubble-area scale. Countries need at least three dated points. All 31 researched countries are accounted for in the inventory, including hidden countries, evidence gaps and the suspended Cyprus series.

Twelve reviewed comparison segments are used. Short dashed connections identify a country across definition or method changes; they do not establish comparability. Missing years are not zero and there are no regional totals. China is a listed-company panel, not a verified national employment total.

All 84 accepted additions have explicit decisions: 49 plotted, 24 withheld for scope, 10 hidden by the country-point filter, and one alternative UK measure omitted.
