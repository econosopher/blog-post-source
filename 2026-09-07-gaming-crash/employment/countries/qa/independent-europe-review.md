# Independent review of European source definitions

Reviewed by the primary researcher on 9 September 2026, independently of regional extraction. This is a targeted review of consequential definitions, revisions and conflicts, supplemented by the regional packet checks.

| Country | Evidence inspected | Assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Germany | 2026 report employment page, visually rendered as `de2026-independent.png` | Chart labels 2025 and 2026 both 12,134 while showing -3%; nearby prose says 12,235. Exclusion of 2026 from preferred charts is justified. |
| Finland | Neogames 2024 original report text and methodology | Domestic 3,800 is explicitly FTE including entrepreneurs; approximately 500 abroad and 4,300 global people remain separate. Sample non-Finnish citizenship share of 37% is separate from adjusted 34-35%. |
| Sweden | 2025 report historical table and company inclusion note | Domestic 2024 count 9,130 verified. Later bankruptcies affect the displayed cohort; latest method break and index exclusion are warranted. |
| Netherlands | Games Monitor 2024 employment and method sections | 4,291 refers to 2023 jobs; 2021 comparator 4,560. Companies moving outside the games scope explain much of the reported change. Non-Dutch passport upper bound is not foreign ownership or a precise point. |
| Serbia | Original 2025 report page 16, visually inspected | Annual respondent counts from 1,281 in 2018 to 3,170 in 2025 are sample totals with changing respondent counts. They remain separate from national workforce estimates. |
| Romania | 2024 release graphic, visually inspected | 6,600 refers to 2023 employees/collaborators. Later 6,100 employee wording is a separate series, not an assumed matching denominator. |
| Poland | PARP/GIC 2025 original PDF pp17-19 and methodology | 14,568 is the 2025 workforce. The graphic's 2,020 foreigners is a count, not a year. Its 'almost 15%' statement differs from 2,020/14,568 = 13.87%; source wording is retained without inventing a precise share. Studio population changes are disclosed. |
| France | EY 2025 sector summary and annual employment chart, original PDF pp54-56 | Sector-specific note excludes independent workers, overriding the generic appendix. Regional researcher corrected definition and retained exact 2019/2024 endpoints separately from the consistently rounded annual chart. |
| Denmark | Regional specialist's independent decoding of June 2026 Tableau workbook and archived editions | Newest workbook has no games extract. 2018, 2021 and 2024 vintages must remain separate because company populations changed. Archive observations added with source locators. |

Spain's annual table and composition denominators were visually checked during regional extraction. Cyprus and Turkey were independently checked by the Asia researcher in `independent-root-review.md`. Remaining uncertainty is retained in each country's limitations, including contractor treatment, source frames and incomplete historical coverage. This review does not promote a company panel or respondent survey into a national census.
