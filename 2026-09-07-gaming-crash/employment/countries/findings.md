# Country games employment: findings

Research cutoff: 9 September 2026. Coverage: 31 countries, with available observations from 2015 onward. Employment periods, report editions and publication dates are recorded separately.

The evidence supports country-specific employment histories. It does not support a single global employment total or a measured transfer of jobs between countries. Recent declines appear in several sources, while other countries report growth. Differences in coverage, units and timing are large enough to change the apparent story.

Four series pass this library's 2019 baseline and continuity checks. Their index values describe changes within each source's population; they do not make the populations equivalent.

The calculations and exact source links are in [indexed_2019.csv](indexed_2019.csv), [citations.csv](citations.csv) and the country notes for [Canada](country-notes/CA.md), [Cyprus](country-notes/CY.md), [Germany](country-notes/DE.md) and [Spain](country-notes/ES.md). Cyprus's administrative categories are imperfect games proxies; Spain's treatment of owners and cross-border direct staff is incompletely specified. These limitations remain attached to the index.

Several recent observations are consistent with contraction in the measured workforce. Canada's ESAC report compares a revised 35,250 FTEs in 2020-21 with 34,010 in 2023-24, a 3.5% decline. UK TIGA counts, including freelancers, fall from 28,516 in May 2024 to 27,347 in September 2025, a 4.1% decline. These are changes in estimated employment stocks, not counts of layoffs. The UK figure also covers production-support roles and does not represent every occupation at games companies. [Canada evidence](country-notes/CA.md); [UK evidence](country-notes/GB.md).

The US source-supported development/publishing subtotal is 61,230 in 2019, 71,991 in 2023 and 60,276 in 2025. Changing mapping methods prevent treating these points as a clean continuous series. The much larger ESA direct-industry totals also include changing amounts of hardware, retail and other activity. Both measures are retained; neither is used in the index. [US definitions and derivations](country-notes/US.md).

Method changes sometimes explain apparent movement. Australia's latest IGEA estimate adds employment estimates for some nonresponding studios. Sweden's latest historical presentation excludes some firms that failed after the employment year. Dutch coverage changes when firms move outside the defined games sector. Denmark's overlapping report vintages use different company populations; the June 2026 dashboard covers film, TV and advertising, leaving 2022 as the latest verified games employment year. Germany's 2026 report contains conflicting text and chart values, so that observation is withheld from preferred charts. [Australia](country-notes/AU.md), [Sweden](country-notes/SE.md), [Netherlands](country-notes/NL.md), [Denmark](country-notes/DK.md), [Germany](country-notes/DE.md).

Asia requires particular care. Korea offers an annual development/publishing series through 2024, but continuity of its population frame has not been fully established. Japan's approximately 58,000-83,000 range includes console hardware and has no verified employment reference year. China's listed-company panel is not a domestic national total; its final observation is an H1 2025 estimate. Vietnam has conflicting estimates, and India's broader gaming estimates have unresolved scope and timing. Malaysia remains a documented national-estimate gap. [Country coverage and source checks](country_coverage.csv).

Composition figures answer narrower questions. Foreign citizenship, origin, a non-Dutch passport, work-visa status and employment abroad are separate measures. Finland's studio survey shares differ from its adjusted national estimate; New Zealand work-visa shares do not measure foreign nationality. Contractor counts are not added to totals that may already include them, and survey percentages are not multiplied into unrelated national employment estimates. [Composition definitions](source_definitions.csv).
