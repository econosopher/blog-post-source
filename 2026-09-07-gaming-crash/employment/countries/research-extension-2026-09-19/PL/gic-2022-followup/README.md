Accepted: **14,200 people working in game production in Poland**, labelled **2022**. Original PolskiGamedev.pl Magazyn vol2 2022/2023, article “Trzy wykresy spowolnienia” by Jakub Marszalkowski, PDF/printed pp140-141. P141 infographic identifies Game Industry Conference as source and 490 producers/publishers. Visually checked against the PDF.

Provenance: author publication record https://www.researchgate.net/publication/367163622_Polski_Gamedev_-_Raport_2022 identifies January2023 and publisher Indie Games Poland. Its public link https://bit.ly/pgdevpubII redirects to https://drive.google.com/file/d/1MQ8HMbRiFoX5phR5PqvzmrIN2M0MhSW6/view . Public original downloaded without authentication or outreach.

## Timing and comparison decision
The article says an increase of approximately2,100 people occurred over15months, contrasting with the previous2,400 increase overone year. Thus the chart's17.3% change from12,110 to14,200 is not a uniform12-month rate. Exact stock day/month is not supplied in the inspected article. Preserve2022 label; do not invent31December. No FTE conversion. Accept marker only, without joining adjacent report vintages.

## Historical estimates
P140 explicitly marks pre-report values with a dashed line. P141 states that employment was not counted before2020 and earlier numbers relied on contemporary circulating opinions, at most adjusted by later knowledge. This is stronger evidence that2016=4000 and2018=6000 are rough reconstructions, not a measured annual workforce series.

## Forward comparison
PARP/GIC2023 gives15,290 for its contemporary snapshot, not14,200, and explicitly permits historical revisions as new information appears (method pp57-59). Its growth narrative is not sufficient to recreate a prior-year denominator. Preserve both source dates and vintages.
