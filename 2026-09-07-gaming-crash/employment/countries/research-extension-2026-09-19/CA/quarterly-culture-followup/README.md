# Canadian interactive-media jobs: quarterly companion

[Chart](interactive-media-quarterly.png) · [Exact source rows](interactive-media-jobs.csv) · [Definition and decisions](series-review.json)

57 quarterly observations,2012Q1-2026Q1, downloaded from Statistics Canada table36-10-0652-01, vectorv1277496929. This is a separate product-perspective, seasonally adjusted jobs series covering games and related interactive digital edutainment. It is not annual ILUs, FTE, or unique people. No quarterly values were inserted into the annual country-observation ledger.

**Break at2016Q1:** Statistics Canada explicitly attributes the2015Q4-2016Q1 discontinuity to improved statistical measurement, not real economic change. The plot uses separate segments. Historical values are one archived current vintage; do not mix vintages or sum quarterly jobs.

The current SEPH annual metadata was also inspected: software publishers and computer-systems services are broad categories, with neither513212 nor541515 available separately. No newer game-only ILU total was recovered.

Sources and methodological limits are recorded in series-review.json and the country source catalog. Further review: full employment allocation/benchmarking method, and whether an annual culture-account counterpart is useful as a separate comparison.
