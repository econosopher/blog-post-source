# Canada: annual interactive-media jobs

[Source table 36-10-0452-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=3610045201) | [Annual CSV](interactive-media-annual-jobs.csv) | [Quarterly companion](../quarterly-culture-followup/README.md)

15 source-native annual observations, 2010-2024. Latest: **34,999 jobs in 2024**. These are product-attributed jobs, not studio headcount or FTE. All 13 overlapping years reconcile to the quarterly average within 0.25 jobs, consistent with integer rounding. Annual and quarterly estimates share a benchmark and are not independent evidence.

**Break at 2016:** changed games and website-design measurement. Use 2010-2015 and 2016-2024 as separate segments; no across-break change calculation.

## How the estimates are made

The [PTCI methods (2022 vintage)](https://www23.statcan.gc.ca/imdb/p2SV.pl?Function=getSurvey&Id=1541863) use Canadian Productivity Accounts jobs, with Labour Force Survey control totals and primarily SEPH industry allocation. The Culture Satellite Account supplies the classification and allocation framework. This is an official statistical estimate of jobs attributable to products, not a count of employees at surveyed game studios. Detailed game-specific allocation ratios were not found in these general methods.

The [NCI methods (Q4 2023 vintage)](https://www23.statcan.gc.ca/imdb/p2SV.pl?Function=getSurvey&Id=1539957) benchmark employment to the annual accounts and use SEPH for quarterly projection. Estimates are seasonally adjusted and revisable; no direct margin of error is available. The [2024 release](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260602/dq260602a-eng.htm) confirms the 2016 refinement.

## Use limits

Jobs include part-time and full-time positions and can exceed people. The source CSV explicitly identifies jobs in units; the mixed-unit table heading is not a multiplier for employment. Keep separate from administrative ILU and ESAC FTE series. This companion is not appended to the main annual comparison ledger.

Archived ZIP, metadata, exact extracted rows, review and annual/quarterly reconciliation are included. Retrieved 19 September 2026.
