## What the dated EY edition resolves

The baseline's 66,000 EY estimate was undated. EY's **December 2023** *New frontiers* provides a dated FY23 factsheet and says its broad online-gaming employment total is approximately **100,000**. The report gives the relevant definition: *direct and indirect roles across core and support functions*. It cites the **TeamLease Digital Gaming Report, November 2022** for that employment claim.

This is evidence for a FY23 broad online-gaming stock, not a replacement for the undated 66,000 baseline series and not evidence that employment rose from 50,000 to 100,000. The 50,000 is labelled direct employment; the EY total is direct plus indirect. In addition, EY says real-money gaming accounted for 82.8% of FY23 online-gaming market value. No subtraction or core-development conversion is justified.

## Backward result

The recoverable historical observation is **2,500 personnel in 2006**, attributed to a NASSCOM report release and limited to around 150 gaming companies. The source also gives 16,500 people across animation and gaming, but that combined-sector count is deliberately excluded. The NASSCOM original was not found, so 2,500 is proposed with secondary-source provenance rather than accepted as a continuous association series.

The later NASSCOM Gaming Forum material found in this bounded sweep describes Applied Games and market context but supplied no dated game-employment stock. NASSCOM's general IT workforce must not be used as a gaming proxy.

## Forward result and exclusions

No source-native, post-FY23 India game-employment reference-year stock with a defined method was located in this bounded source-family sweep. The 2025 Primus 130,000 claim remains undated in the baseline and is not revised here.

Excluded forecasts include NASSCOM's 13,000-by-2010 projection, TeamLease's FY23 jobs-addition claim, and EY/Kalaari's 250,000-by-2025 claim. They are not observations. `proposed-observations.json` contains additions only; no shared catalog or accepted-observation file was edited.
