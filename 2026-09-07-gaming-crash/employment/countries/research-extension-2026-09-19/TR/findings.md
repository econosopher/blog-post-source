# Turkey: employment archive extension

This pass **reconfirms only the existing 2023 TOGED/EGDF observation: 10,777 FTE-oriented workers**. It does not add an earlier or later stock. EGDF labels the chart as “Number of people working in the industry,” while its questionnaire defines the requested employment measure as FTE employees, entrepreneurs and in-house freelancers of game-developer studios and third-party publishers.

The geography is not strictly domestic. EGDF directs associations to include remote employees in third countries when the studio is registered in Turkey; it excludes foreign subsidiaries and foreign subcontractor companies. The questionnaire excludes service providers that are not also a developer studio or publisher. TOGED's country-specific sample, gross-up, reference date and FTE conversion are not published. Treat 10,777 as a qualified association snapshot, not a residents-only workforce census and not a trend anchor.

The Turkey chart shows **N/A** employment for 2019-2022. Its nearby **3,300** belongs to the turnover series (million euros), not employment. The 2023 chart also shows 522 developer studios and €1,016m turnover; neither is a worker count.

Gaming in Türkiye's primary archive lists annual market reports from 2016 through 2025. The accessible 2021 original was searched for employment language and provides no national workforce stock. Current 2023-2025 reports are gated behind an email form. The archive therefore establishes potential source coverage, not an employment series. No value was inferred from studio counts, investment, revenue or narrative references to jobs.

TÜİK's labour-force product is authoritative for broad economic activity, but its public labour release groups information/communication activity with telecommunications, programming, consultancy, infrastructure and other information services. It is not a digital-games measure. The public dataset interface did not expose an extractable games-specific table in this bounded pass. Do not use broad software or information-sector employment as a games proxy.

The extension saves the decisive EGDF PDF and extracted text in `raw/`, alongside the market-report archive and TÜİK interface shell. The source-visit log records access and recovery next steps. No secondary 2,500-3,000 claim is promoted: its cited original has still not been verified.
