# Turkey recheck: association and conference employment claims

## Result

This recheck recovered two additional *published claims*, but neither supplies a survey frame, collection cadence, reference date, respondent universe, or calculation method. They must not be joined to the existing EGDF/TOGED 2023 marker as a time series.

| Published source | Dated claim | Scope stated by source | Exact locator | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EGDF, *How To Enable Digital Growth in Europe?* (PDF metadata: 12 Nov 2015; third-party preserved copy) | **5,000 people working in game development in Turkey** | Map is headed "Number of people working in game development." It does not define employers, geography, FTE/headcount, or data year. | PDF p. 4 (printed p. 4), Turkey label on the map | A historically dated *publication claim*, not a dated employment observation. Keep out of the plotted annual series unless EGDF or TOGED provides the underlying 2015 data date and method. |
| TOBB Türkiye Kreatif Endüstriler Meclisi and TOGED, *Oyun Ekosistemi Değerlendirme Toplantısı Sonuç Raporu*, meeting 4 Mar 2026 | **25,000 directly employed / qualified employment in game development** | The report calls this the producer-side game-development sector. It excludes the consumer-side ecosystem, but does not say whether publishers, overseas staff, contractors, or part-time staff are included. | PDF p. 5 executive summary; pp. 12–13 (printed pp. 12–13), especially p. 13: “Üretici tarafında ise 25 bin kişinin doğrudan istihdam edildiği...” | A dated association/conference assertion with a clear high-level producer-side scope but no measurement date or method. Do not turn it into a 2026 stock or connect it to 2023=10,777. |

The 2026 report itself confirms the method limitation: its quantified work is a 15-question live Mentimeter survey of roughly 52 meeting participants about the proposed law, not a workforce survey (PDF p. 5, pp. 41–48 / printed pp. 41–48). It explicitly calls for employment data to be centrally and systematically collected in a future database (PDF p. 67 / printed p. 67, and p. 73 / printed p. 73). This is strong evidence that 25,000 is not accompanied here by an enumerated national census or survey.

## Checks that delimit the history

* EGDF's primary *European Report on the Game Development Industry in 2018* (published Aug 2020) labels the Turkey 2018 employment entry **N/A** on PDF p. 4 (printed p. 2). Its method note says national association sources can be surveys, public registers or estimates, and marks estimates with an asterisk. `N/A` means EGDF had no data collected from that country (PDF p. 2 / printed p. 1). This means **no reportable 2018 Turkey workforce value**, not zero employment.
* EGDF's 2020 country panel published in 2022 records Turkey/TOGED with 52 studios and **N/A** people working for 2016 (PDF p. 60 / printed p. 60). This is a second absent-data cell, not a workforce estimate. It does not corroborate or disprove the unmethoded 2015 map claim.
* The pre-existing packet's 2023 EGDF/TOGED point remains the strongest scoped source: **10,777 FTE-oriented people working**, but it has a mixed registration geography and missing Turkey-specific frame, date and gross-up method. See parent packet `TR/proposed-observations.json` and `TR/raw/egdf2023.pdf` p. 57.
* Public original Gaming in Türkiye 2019 and 2022 market reports were downloaded and searched. Neither gives a national game-workforce stock. They contain qualitative employment discussion and individual-company/team statements only. They are organized annual reports, but not employment series sources.
* The TOGED 4 Mar 2026 meeting presentation was also checked. It contains legislative material and no workforce count.

## What is missing versus inaccessible

**Missing from accessed sources:** a recurring TOGED/TOBB/TOYED/official workforce survey; reference dates; employer register or respondent list; response rate; gross-up; FTE/full-time/contract treatment; domestic versus registered-employer geography; and a reproducible annual series.

**Not established as absent:** removed, private, unindexed or non-web TOGED/TOBB records; the underlying data behind the 2015 EGDF map; and the source/method behind the 2026 25,000 assertion. The current evidence only supports saying those details were not published in the accessed reports.
