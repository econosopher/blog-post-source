# Vietnam game-industry employment archive

Research date: 2026-09-19. Scope: a bounded public-source sweep for an annual Vietnam game-industry employment stock. The destination country catalog was deliberately not opened and no duplicate check against it was possible.

## Decisive finding

The strongest usable observation is **approximately 4,100 game-industry workers in 2024**. Vietnam's Ministry of Information and Communications (MIC) published it on 2024-12-29, attributing the estimate to the Authority of Broadcasting and Electronic Information (ABEI): “Số lượng lao động ngành game năm 2024 ước khoảng 4.100 người, tăng 31% so với năm 2023.” The same article says the value was reported at ABEI's 2024 electronic-information activity review meeting.

This is an **estimated annual employment stock**, not jobs created, vacancies, learners, players, esports audience, or an employment forecast. The available official wording does not provide a statistical frame, collection method, a precise reference date, or a formal inclusion rule. It uses “ngành game” / game industry; the surrounding text refers to game production and publishing firms, licensing, and sector governance. It is reasonable to label the scope **Vietnam game industry (developers/publishers/associated sector labour; exact boundary unstated)** and retain the caveat rather than silently recast it as game-development-only employment.

## Series treatment

Proposed series: `VNM_game_industry_employment_estimated`.

* 2024: 4,100 persons, annual estimate, source-published estimate.
* Do **not** create a 2023 level from the stated 31% growth. Back-calculation gives about 3,130, while a secondary translation reports “about 3,140”; the government source does not publish a 2023 headcount. Keep the growth statement as a non-level comparison only.
* Do **not** substitute VNG's 1,200 employees. It is a company figure, may span group functions and locations, and cannot represent national game employment.
* Do **not** substitute official job-board vacancies, GameVerse attendance, esports audiences, or training enrolment; they measure different populations.

## Corroboration and limits

The current official online-game portal of ABEI repeats the 4,100/31% wording in a 2026 republication of the 2024 meeting material. VietnamPlus/VietnamNet also reported the figure and attributed it to MIC. These are corroboration only; MIC's 2024 page is the preferred source record.

The National Statistics Office Labour Force Survey is an authoritative national employment source, but the visit found no game-specific series. It should not be used to manufacture a sector observation without a published industry cross-tab.
