# Indonesia employment archive extension - 19 September 2026

## Result

Two additional authoritative Indonesian-government series were located. Neither is a clean game-development national trend.

1. **2019 survey respondent total: 1,286 people.** The official *Peta Ekosistem Industri Game 2020* says this is the number of people involved in the game industry in its survey: 1,136 in incorporated game developers and 150 in unincorporated developers. The report's headline repeats 1,136, but that is the incorporated subtotal and must not be used as the all-respondent figure. This is not a national estimate: the report used a non-probability survey, a census attempt for incorporated firms, and snowball sampling from community databases for unincorporated developers. Its stated focus is 2019; data collection took place in 2020. The worker-status graphic distinguishes permanent, contract and internship workers, but the extracted total does not publish a total by status. The study covers game developers; it does not establish whether a publisher-only population is included in the 1,286 total.

2. **2013 national policy-sector estimate: 23,928 people.** The government *Rencana Pengembangan Permainan Interaktif Nasional 2015-2019* reports that the interactive-games subsector absorbed up to 23,928 workers in 2013, after presenting a 2010-2013 series. This is much broader than domestic video-game developers: its definition covers games across electronic, online, physical, card and board media, and the policy document says the then-available 2009 creative KBLI classifications did not fully match its proposed scope. Treat as an older, broad policy-subsector national estimate only; it is not comparable to the 2019 developer survey or the 2024 developer/publisher lower bound.

## Baseline continuity and editions

- The existing baseline's 2024 source remains the latest official mapping: it reports **at least 2,112 talents at video-game developers and publishers**, but provides no public sampling details or exact observation date. It also reports 513,000-1.03 million economy-wide workers affected, which remains an impact range, not core industry employment.
- The 2020 mapping explicitly says it compares itself with a 2015 Kominfo study, *Peta Industri Game Indonesia 2015*. The original 2015 publication was identified through the citation trail and former IGRS URL, but no accessible official full text or dated employment figure was retrieved in this pass. Do not reverse the 2024 stated 64.23% growth to create a 2015 workforce value.
- The 2021 Kominfo/AGI/Niko Partners edition was located and reviewed at high level. It adds market, gamer and company information; this pass found no additional employment stock suitable for extraction.

## Recommended handling

- Keep 2019 1,286 only as a **survey-respondent snapshot** (not chart-comparable national employment). The incorporated 1,136 and unincorporated 150 values are additive components and must not be separately summed with the all-developer total.
- Keep 2013 23,928 in a separate **broad interactive-games policy subsector** series, excluded from core game-development comparisons and trends.
- Do not add a 2015 observation until the original report or a source that reproduces its methodology and precise value is retrieved.

## Primary-source locators

- 2015-2019 policy document, pp. 63-65: 2010-2013 employment discussion; p. 73: 2013 value 23,928; pp. 4-5 and 51-52: broad interactive-game scope and classification caveat.
