## Coverage
2007 foreword p2 uses business-registry accounts and latest statements for non-calendar fiscal years. P8 excludes non-limited and foreign-registered entities, hobby projects and some development in companies whose principal activity is different. Missing accounts are explicitly listed. 2008 repeats this coverage limitation. 2009 p5 includes game-specialist technology/content suppliers, advertising and serious games; excludes e-learning without game elements, gambling, publishers, pure distributors and retailers. Four missing accounts are listed; p2 notes both bankruptcies and removal of companies no longer primarily developing games. Thus its reported decline is not a fixed-panel employment change.

## First-edition period conflict
The recovered first edition is titled Spelutvecklarindex 2005, publication series 1-07. Its p3 foreword calls 812.5 the 2005 workforce, but p4 labels 715 as 2004/2005 and 812.5 as 2005/2006. The 2007 table relabels the same totals 2005 and 2006; 2008/2009 repeat 812.5 under 2006. These are not independent observations. Neither has been assigned a single calendar year in the accepted annual ledger.

The first edition also has a component inconsistency: 702.25 men plus 107.25 women equals 809.5, not its headline 812.5. This does not justify altering the published total.

Next: seek an explicit retrospective domestic split and clarification of early fiscal-period labels. This is a documented source limitation, not evidence of zero employment or missing industry activity.
