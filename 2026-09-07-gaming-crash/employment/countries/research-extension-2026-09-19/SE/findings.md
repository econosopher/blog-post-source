# Sweden archive extension

Accepted three historical observations from the publisher's [2020 report](https://dataspelsbranschen.squarespace.com/s/15122020_GDI_EN_48p_digital_s_below20.pdf): **1,967 (2012), 2,534 (2013), 3,117 (2014)**. The employment chart explicitly distinguishes Sweden-based and overseas employment. Primary researcher visually checked PDF page 13, printed page 10.

Keep this as a separate report vintage: annual-account averages are supplemented with December counts where available; freelancers and subcontractors absent from company accounts are not counted. These are not uniform year-end headcounts. The older 2018 report's 2016/2017 headlines include overseas staff, which the 2020 report splits out. Do not import those headline values as domestic employment.

The publisher archive currently lists editions through 2025. It explains that numbering switched to publication year with the 2014 edition, so no missing 2013 edition should be inferred. Earlier reports are available for further backward tracing. Failed old-domain links and the successful archive-host route are recorded in `source-visits.json`.

## Overlap check

The 2020 and 2025 report vintages agree for 2016–2019 in the retained packets, but their 2015 figures differ: 3,709 versus 3,439. This is evidence against silently splicing older values into the latest-vintage series. The accepted 2012–2014 rows retain the 2020 source vintage and its geographic labels; no claim is made that later retrospective revisions leave them unchanged. See `overlap-vintage-check.csv`.
