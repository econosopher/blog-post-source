## Exact primary locators

- **2017:** RGDA post [Despre Dev.Play si sprijinul industriei locale de game development](https://rgda.ro/despre-dev-play-si-sprijinul-industriei-locale-de-game-development/), dated 7 August 2017, paragraph beginning *"Repet cifrele"*: 140M USD turnover, around 80 companies, and around 6,000 employees. It does not state the observation year, method, or collaborator treatment.
- **2020:** [RGDA Report 2020](https://rgda.ro/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/RGDA-Report-2020_ffinal.pdf), PDF p. 3, Executive Summary: *"6000+ employees"*. The associated RGDA report page is dated 29 October 2021. The original PDF is retained as `raw/rgda-report-2020-full.pdf`.
- **2021:** [RGDA's General Assembly](https://rgda.ro/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/RGDAs-General-Assembly.pdf), PDF p. 14 / printed slide 13, slide *"2021 snapshot of Romania’s video games industry"*, dated October 2022: *"Employees and collaborators: 6,700"* and *"+12% versus 2020"*. The original image-first PDF is retained as `raw/rgda-general-assembly-2023.pdf`; its page text is independently indexed by the source search result.

## Scope and contractor check

The 2020 report is the clearest scope evidence, but it does not make the national headline reproducible. It says the report was prepared from public material, industry discussions, and member forms and was not audited. It separately says an Employee/Collaborator Survey at the beginning of 2021 had 37 responding companies.

That survey's workforce-status chart is composition evidence only: 94.7% full-time employees or collaborators, 3.9% project-based collaborators, and 1.71% part-time collaborators or employees. It neither gives a national numerator for each group nor demonstrates that the 6,000+ headline uses the same frame. Contractor/freelance workers are therefore explicitly in scope for the 2021, 2022, and 2023 *employees/collaborators* points, but the split is unknown. The 2024 switch to *employees* does not prove a contractor exclusion; its contractor treatment is unresolved.

## Headcount versus financial reporting

RGDA states that **2020 revenue** was reported to tax authorities. It does not make the same claim for headcount, so the employment series must not be presented as company-financial-statement employment or as government payroll data.

The 2023 snapshot reports EUR317m turnover, 6,600 employees/collabs, and EUR48,154 turnover per employee. Dividing the first by the last yields approximately 6,583 people, which is consistent with a rounded displayed headcount. The 2024 report's 6,100 employees and -7.4% year-on-year rate imply an unreported prior denominator of approximately 6,588. These checks explain why 6,600 to 6,100 is not exactly -7.4%; they are **not** primary headcount observations and must not replace the published rounded values.

## Retrospective revision check

No later RGDA primary source located in this bounded pass republishes an exact revised 2021, 2022, or 2023 headcount. The 2024 report's -7.4% is compatible with an unexposed, unrounded 2023 denominator, rather than evidence of a retrospective revision. Keep published values as displayed and do not infer missing annual points.

## Government check and limits

No dedicated Romanian government/INSSE game-development employment series was found in the bounded primary search. Broad CAEN/IT series would cover populations that are materially broader than RGDA's studio compilation. The archive therefore supports an association-defined, non-audited industry series only, with clear breaks between the early employee/lower-bound language, the 2021-2023 collaborator-inclusive language, and 2024's employee wording.

## Main-agent acceptance

2020 and 2021 accepted after inspecting the original PDF. The 2020 display remains `6,000+`; no strict greater-than interpretation is imposed. The 2017 statement remains contextual because its underlying reference year is unstated. Slide 13 is PDF page 14.
