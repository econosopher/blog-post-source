# New Zealand archive extension

Four earlier source-native survey snapshots accepted: September2011 **359FTE**; March2012 **380FTE**; March2013 **448FTE**; March2014 **450FTE**. The association surveyed21,33,33 and30 studios respectively. See [metadata](accepted-observations.json) and [inspection log](source-visits.json).

Three traps matter: the2013 release title says2012, but the body twice dates the figures31March2013; the2011 boilerplate says over360 whereas the explicit survey result is359; and the2015 release claims134 additional jobs to568, implying434 rather than the2014 published450. Do not silently revise450 or interpret this as a consistent panel. The2014 report also refers separately to several hundred part-time developers without explaining their FTE inclusion.

The2026 preliminary announcement supplies revenue, not a new workforce count. The report says fuller statistics will follow in September; no2026 employment value is accepted.

A promising independent source is MBIE's Game Development Sector Rebate Year Two Review, with a linked data workbook. Indexed official text describes a linked-business-data cohort formed from166 NZGDA-listed firms. Both direct report and workbook requests returned403. This is an access failure, not absence of data. Full methodology and cells are still required before accepting its historical employment charts; cohort selection and possible survivorship need explicit checks.

Use the accepted association values as dated scale snapshots, not an uninterrupted national annual index. Do not infer2010 employment by subtracting reported growth.
