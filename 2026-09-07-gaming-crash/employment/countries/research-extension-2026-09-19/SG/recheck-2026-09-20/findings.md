# Singapore recheck: annual-report and official-manpower trace

## Result

The only usable national games-employment observation remains the existing **2021, nearly 2,000 people** from MDDI's parliamentary answer. It is an explicitly broad games-sector snapshot: the answer frames games together with esports and does not isolate a developer/publisher-only denominator or state worker-status treatment. The answer expressly says the separate **2,700** figure is an indicative future manpower-demand estimate from the 2019 survey, not realised employment.

No usable Singapore time series was found in this pass. It remains a one-point context measure, not a trend.

## SGGA annual-report lead

SGGA's public home page says it issues annual data reports on the Singapore games industry. Its publicly accessible sitemap exposes no reports/data page and no downloadable annual report was found in focused public searches. A later SGGA-linked public update describes the industry survey/data as being refreshed, with data still rough and under refinement. This supports a publication gap, not a count.

The association is a necessary source family to revisit when it makes reports public, but the public evidence reviewed here does not support constructing an annual workforce series.

## IMDA series limit

The public IMDA Tech and Media Talent workbook reviewed in the earlier archive pass tracks broad technology/media workforce measures for 2020-2025. It has no games-only employment row, occupational breakdown, or SSIC 58201/62012 series. It cannot be proportioned into games employment. The MDDI answer identifies these SSIC codes as the development/publishing-company population, which makes them the best precise lead for a future released administrative table.

## Use

- Keep the existing 2021 nearly-2,000 MDDI observation, clearly labelled "games sector, esports context".
- Do not plot 2,700 as employment.
- Do not use SGGA's annual-report claim, member directory, company count, survey-refresh announcement, or IMDA broad tech/media totals as employment observations.
- Do not draw a Singapore trend until a released SGGA or IMDA source supplies repeatable, defined games workforce values.
