## Continuity and limits

The sources consistently separate development/publishing from the PC-cafe and arcade-venue workforce, so their sector label matches the present KR_core definition. They are national annual survey estimates reported in people. The available material does not establish survey-frame continuity, contractor treatment, or the onset of later frame exclusions. Treat the recovered history as vintage-separated and not trend-eligible until methods are audited.

The 2013 and 2014 values are reproduced in a government-hosted table which identifies its source as the KOCCA 2015 White Paper. The direct KOCCA file for that edition was not recovered during this bounded pass. 2014 is independently confirmed in direct KOCCA material; 2013 was initially supported by one-step-removed provenance and is now directly confirmed by a KOCCA-issued release (see follow-up below).
