### China: what the decline actually means

The source is **CNG / Gamma Data**, not the National People's Congress. This standalone report is also distinct from the trade association's annual China Game Industry Report. Its page 8 shows personnel at major listed game companies: **223,000 in 2021 and 205,800 in 2024**, a source-panel decline of **8.6%**. Constituents, consistent-panel rules, domestic versus overseas allocation and game-business staffing allocation are not disclosed. It does not establish a national Chinese games-employment decline. [Original CNG report, pp. 1–2 and 8](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202507181711204583_1.pdf?1752858662000.pdf=).

The H1 2025 value of 202,800 is estimated and separate from the annual sequence. The regional figure now marks it separately. The earlier roughly 1.45m workforce claim has different, unrecovered counting methods and is not joined to this panel.
