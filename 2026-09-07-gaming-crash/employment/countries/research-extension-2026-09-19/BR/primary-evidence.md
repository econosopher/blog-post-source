# Brazil: primary evidence extract

Research date: 2026-09-19. These are exact source locators and short, necessary extracts from public primary reports. They support the proposed observations and their limits.

## 2005 Abragames mapping

- Original: [A Indústria de Desenvolvimento de Jogos Eletrônicos no Brasil](https://www.abragames.org/uploads/5/6/8/0/56805537/pesquisa_2005_-_a_industria_de_desenvolvimento_de_jogos_eletronicos_no_brasil.pdf), published 1 May 2005.
- Scope: printed p. 4 says the research covers developer companies, excluding distribution, marketing and advertising; fieldwork was 21 February-3 April.
- Employment locator: printed p. 12, section 2.6, says the average number of `funcionários` in game companies was approximately 15 people.
- Handling: no total workforce observation. The report gives an average rather than a denominator-compatible total, does not say whether founders are included, and does not establish contractor treatment.

## 2008 Abragames mapping

- Original: [A indústria brasileira de jogos eletrônicos](https://www.abragames.org/uploads/5/6/8/0/56805537/pesquisa_2008_-_a_industria_brasileira_de_jogos_eletronicos.pdf), July 2008.
- Scope: printed p. 2 says the study focuses on domestic production for local and export markets.
- Employment locator: printed p. 3: `560 profissionais ... empregados por 42 empresas que produzem software para jogos eletrônicos`.
- Method limit: printed p. 11 thanks 32 responding companies. The report does not explain whether the 42-company employment headline is a census, a mapped universe, or an expansion from the 32 respondents. It also does not establish founder or contractor treatment.

## 2014 BNDES-funded I Census

- Original: [I Censo da Indústria Brasileira de Jogos Digitais](https://www.abragames.org/uploads/5/6/8/0/56805537/i_censo_da_industria_brasileira_de_jogos_digitais_2.pdf), GEDIGames/NPGT Escola Politécnica USP for BNDES, presented July 2014.
- Method locator: printed pp. 31-32. The online questionnaire ran 9-31 January 2014; it used developer contact lists, association outreach, social-network distribution and telephone follow-up. It received 135 complete questionnaires and retained 133 valid responses after duplicate removal.
- Employment locator: printed p. 39, section 4.3.2: `o número total de trabalhadores nestas 133 empresas foi de 1.133. Destes, 392 são sócios e 741 são colaboradores.`
- Interpretation: 1,133 is an observed respondent-company workforce stock. It includes partners/founders and collaborators, and it must not be presented as an extrapolated national total. The report's 2013 revenue table is a separate reference period.

## Later source chain

- The existing 2018 II Census record is a government-funded successor with a different basis: the extant packet distinguishes 2,731 respondent people from an extrapolated developer-workforce total.
- The existing 2022 and 2023 Abragames/Brazil Games surveys estimate workforce by mapping firms and extrapolating respondent mean employment. They are not a mechanically comparable continuation of the 2008 or 2014 respondent figures.
- [Nordicity's June 2024 white paper](https://brazilgames.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/nordicity_final-1.pdf), printed p. 17, states that the industry employed about 13,225 people in 2023 and cites Abragames (2023). This is a repeat citation, not a 2024 measurement.
