# Fortaleza regional census review

Checked 19 September 2026. No national or regional employment-stock point accepted.

The original Censo Fortaleza Game Dev 2024 is by the collective Da Cidade à Tela, through Centro Cultural Bom Jardim research laboratory, with Instituto Dragão do Mar support. ASCENDE Jogos and IGDA Fortaleza are thanked. It is community research with public cultural-institution support, not a government statistical census. Credits date publication to 2026; the questionnaire distribution table records 7, 14, 21 and 28 October 2024. Do not label the workforce reference year 2026.

Pages 19-20 describe a 74-question Google Forms survey, piloted with five professionals, promoted through IGDA Discord/Instagram, the Ceará games WhatsApp forum, association company emails and the pilot group. Target: Fortaleza/metropolitan residents or workers at Fortaleza companies. There are 101 responses, not an estimated employment total. The authors acknowledge channel selection, nonresponse and self-report limitations. Page 28 records 11 respondents living in the interior, so this is not strictly a city-resident sample.

The report compares its 101 responses to approximately 200 people in a separate 2023 statewide mapping to suggest 50.5% coverage. This is not a validated response rate: different geography, community eligibility and a non-exhaustive predecessor mapping prevent assuming a common population frame.

Page 31 visually checked: all-respondent games-income categories are 61 zero, 13 up to one minimum wage, 19 one-to-three, 8 three-to-six, 0 above six; total101. The five-largest-occupations cross-tab has89 people (41+21+18+6+3), including56 with zero games income (25+15+10+4+2). Therefore56 is a subset count, not the overall zero-income numerator. Neither101 nor the40 with nonzero games income is a valid employed-worker stock. Household zero income is4 overall and3 in that five-role subset. Do not treat these correctly different denominators as contradictory totals.

Backward trace: Ceará Jogos links a 2023 statewide MPJ-CE mapping; Sebrae's public description explicitly includes digital and analog games, businesses, individuals and education initiatives. It is therefore not automatically comparable to this digital-games community survey. PDF retrieval of that mapping failed (HTTP406; web screenshots timed out), so its numerical results remain unverified. The current archive lists the2024 census; no later edition was located in this bounded check.

Decision: retain as regional working-conditions evidence. Do not connect a2023 statewide mapping count to101 survey respondents or put either into Brazil's national employment line. Recover the2023 mapping only if needed for separate regional context; national source gaps remain higher priority.
