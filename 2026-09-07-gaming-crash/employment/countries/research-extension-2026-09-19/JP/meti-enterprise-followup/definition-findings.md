# METI game-software employment definition review

## Decision

FY2017, FY2018 and FY2020 can be described as the **same reported measure in
changing-respondent snapshots**: `常時従業者数` for the development/production
division of responding game-software enterprises. This is not a national employment
series or a basis for a trend claim.

FY2010 cannot be called definition-identical to that post-2018 group. It remains a
proposed historical snapshot, with no direct level-change comparison to FY2017+
approved.

## What METI changed in 2018

METI's 2021 overview, p. 23 note 1, says that the definition of
`正社員・正職員以外（パート・アルバイトなど）` changed beginning with the 2018
(H30) survey, which reports FY2017 results, and cautions comparisons to FY2016 and
earlier. The warning identifies the **non-regular subcategory**, not the total
`常時従業者数`.

The H30 Table 7 already has the post-change layout: the total, then regular
employees, part-time/albaito *excluding contract employees*, transferees, and
contract employees *including freelancers*. FY2018 has the identical layout, and
the FY2020 questionnaire gives the same total-versus-separate-temporary/incoming-
dispatch structure. This supports continuity for the total across FY2017/FY2018/
FY2020; it does not make their respondent pools fixed.

## FY2010 is a separate break

The FY2010 rapid release defines `常時従業者` as paid directors and regular
employees, regardless of title, with a contract **over one month** or with at least
18 days of employment in each of the two months preceding fiscal-year end/nearest
accounting period. It excludes temporary/daily workers and excludes incoming
dispatched workers from the receiving firm's total.

The post-2018 guide instead defines the total as paid directors and workers hired
without a fixed term or for **one month or more**. Both formulations exclude
temporary employees and incoming dispatch workers, but their eligibility tests are
not word-for-word equivalent: the earlier 18-days-in-each-of-two-months route
disappears, while a contract of exactly one month is explicit later. No official
game-software bridge or recast was found. The evidence therefore does not support
calling FY2010 and FY2017+ the same total definition.
