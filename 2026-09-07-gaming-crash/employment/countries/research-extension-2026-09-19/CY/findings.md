# Cyprus games-employment archive — bounded research

Research date: 2026-09-19. Geography: Republic of Cyprus / Cyprus national statistics unless stated otherwise.

## Result

No additional, non-duplicative national video-games workforce observation was found for inclusion.

The accessible predecessor to CYGMA's *Cyprus Video Game Industry Report 2025* is C Business Advisors' *Cyprus Games Industry Footprint 2024* (published 24 July 2026). It displays the same 2019–2024 employment sequence already supplied in the CYGMA baseline. It labels the series “Total persons employed in publishing,” and states that it cannot determine which employees are based in Cyprus and which work remotely abroad. It therefore cannot be substituted for CYGMA's local tax-paying-games-employment definition, and is not duplicated in `proposed-observations.json`.

CYGMA describes the 2025 report as the inaugural / first measured national account. Its news archive through 19 September 2026 shows no later employment edition or dated 2025/2026 actual workforce result. The report's methodology identifies the Footprint as a contributor, rather than an independent later series.

## CYSTAT detailed-activity check

The current CYSTAT National Accounts annual table supplies employment in persons by NACE, but its smallest relevant activity is J58 “Publishing activities” and J62–63 combined; it has no software-publishing (NACE 58.2/58.21) or video-game-publishing cell. Its 2024 and 2025 values are marked provisional. The current Labour Force Survey table is broader still: its information categories are J and K-style sections rather than games/software-publishing detail.

The historical CYSTAT *Business Services 2013* publication does show “Software publishing” in its activity taxonomy, but its published employment table suppresses the split by reporting a combined `581+582` (books/periodicals/other publishing plus software publishing) total of 1,077 persons engaged. This is broad publishing, not a video-games count, and does not yield a software-publishing observation. The apparent `7` adjacent to that row is the working-proprietor count for NACE 58 as a whole, not a 58.2 employment total.

## Exclusions retained for audit

- C Business Advisors 2019–2024 chart: duplicate values, incompatible stated scope (publishing; location unknown).
- CYGMA 2019–2024 baseline: already provided; not repeated.
- CYSTAT J58/J62, ICT/J, and LFS information-sector totals: broader than games; excluded.
- CYSTAT 2013 `581+582`: combined broad publishing; excluded.
- Company claims (for example GDEV's consolidated 589 employees at 2024 year end, and press reporting of a former Wargaming workforce): company/global or single-company scope, not a Cyprus national workforce series; excluded.
- Forecasts, report-title years, company counts and turnover/GVA figures: not dated actual workforce observations.

## Recommended next step

Suspend the 2019–2024 series from comparable domestic games-employment use pending reconciliation. CYGMA p15 calls it local tax-paying game-publishing employment, while its cited C Business Advisors predecessor p9 labels the identical numbers publishing employment with worker location unknown. The agreement in values is not independent validation of geography or games-only scope. If an additional series becomes necessary, request the underlying CYSTAT extract / C Business Advisors entity-to-NACE audit and a clear residence, payroll, contractor, and remote-worker definition before adding it.
