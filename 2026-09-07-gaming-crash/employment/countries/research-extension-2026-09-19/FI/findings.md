# Finland: Neogames employment history extension

Research date: 2026-09-19. Scope: five public Neogames report editions (2016, 2018, 2020, 2022, 2024). All PDFs in this folder are the publisher's original public files.

## Finding

There is an explicit domestic FTE series for **end-2022 (approximately 3,700 FTE)** and **end-2024 (approximately 3,800 FTE)**. Both editions separately give Finnish-owned studios' overseas staff: **400 people** in 2022 and **500 people** in 2024. Their global Finnish-studio totals are **4,100 people** and **4,300 people**, respectively. Keep FTE domestic measures and overseas people measures separate; the publisher does not establish that those units are interchangeable.

The source defines foreign-office staff by workplace (Finnish-owned studios abroad), not citizenship. Its non-Finnish-worker percentages concern employees working in Finnish studios in Finland and must not be applied to either national FTE total or overseas staff.

## Method notes

- 2016: 140 surveyed companies of 250 total; source says all largest companies were included. Other available sources confirm the total.
- 2018: 140 interviews, October 2018-February 2019, augmented by other sources; all largest companies included.
- 2020: 134 interviews, October 2020-February 2021, supplemented by other sources; Neogames says these cover well over 90% of industry employment.
- 2022: 134 interviews plus comprehensive review/other sources; 58% of 232 studios, but a clear majority of employees and nearly all turnover.
- 2024: 71 interviews plus public-source employment data for 185 studios; all major studios participated, but the interviewed-company count falls sharply from 2022.

## Gaps and handling

Do not manufacture a domestic 2016 or 2018 number. The reports say their overall totals include abroad but do not supply a sufficiently precise foreign-office component. For 2020, retain 3,350 only as an explicitly labelled derived approximate FTE if it is useful to show the bridge; do not treat it as an observed domestic statistic.
