# Finland recheck: cadence and historic companion series

Neogames says in the 2024 report introduction (printed p. 3) that it has conducted a Finnish Game Industry study **almost every other year since 2004**. It is therefore a recurring biennial/irregular study family, not an annual survey. The 2024 historic chart contains ten published points: 2004, 2008, 2010, 2012, 2014, 2016, 2018, 2020, 2022, and 2024.

`historical-worldwide-observations.json` transcribes every chart value with its report locator. This is useful as a **separate long-horizon Neogames Finnish-studio history**, never as the domestic-country comparison series.

The source's geography changes its use:

- 2016 explicitly includes entrepreneurs and employees working abroad, while excluding interns and freelancers.
- 2018 calls the total FTE and includes entrepreneurs and people employed abroad, without an overseas count.
- 2020 calls the total FTE, includes entrepreneurs and approximately 250 people abroad. Do not subtract people from FTE.
- 2022 and 2024 give explicit domestic FTE and overseas-people components, while the headline totals are people including entrepreneurs.
- For 2004-2014 the current historic chart supplies retrospective values, but this recheck has not independently established each original-vintage worker and geography definition.

Recommended presentation: a labelled companion line or dotted historical context series titled "Finnish-studio employment worldwide (Neogames; entrepreneurs and overseas staff included)". Keep the domestic comparison chart as the direct domestic pair: approximately 3,700 FTE (2022) and 3,800 FTE (2024). Do not splice either series into the other.
