# Chart contract

Audience: readers comparing the scale and measured history of national games employment. Each chart explains its own measurement, unit, observation periods and evidence limits.

One or two source-defined series per country image. Survey composition has separate images and a fixed 0-100% axis. Employment panels start at zero. Sparse observations remain points; lines connect only consecutive observed years within a trend-eligible series and never cross a declared break. Forecasts and non-preferred revisions are retained in CSV but excluded from figures. Ranges remain intervals; no midpoint is estimated. An undated estimate uses a report-edition label, never a fabricated observation year.

The indexed comparison requires a verified 2019 value, at least three observed years, an affirmative comparability assessment, domestic geography, and no unresolved break. It shows within-series change, not equivalent country definitions. Missing years remain gaps. Do not sum countries or infer layoffs, hiring or migration from stocks.

Exports: PNG at 2160 by 1800 pixels and SVG with live text. Direct value labels, muted blue marks, orange break markers, restrained grid, no decorative icons. Each chart links to plotted_values.csv through a chart_id; that table links every mark to its observation and source. Full definitions and source locators remain in the accompanying country notes.

Acceptance: validate foreign keys, periods, units, range ordering, duplicate preferred values, source snapshots, search routes and indexed arithmetic; visually inspect all final figures through legible contact sheets and full-size checks for dense or ambiguous charts.
