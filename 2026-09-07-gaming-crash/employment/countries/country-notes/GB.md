# United Kingdom

TIGA provides production-role FTE snapshots from 2016 to May 2024 and workforce counts including freelancers through September 2025. DCMS supplies a separate 2015-2025 annual jobs series, subject to substantial sampling uncertainty. Ukie surveys provide contractor and nationality shares; changing recruitment prevents treating share changes as national workforce changes.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### Development and production-support full-time equivalent roles (GB_tiga_fte)

Unit: FTE. Population: national_estimate. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** UK studios, publishers, publisher studios, development-service companies and games divisions of broadcasters
- **occupation scope:** Production, QA, support, localisation and technical roles; excludes HR/admin/finance/sales/marketing/commercial staff not directly in production
- **geography note:** Domestic operations, including foreign-owned companies
- **worker status:** Full-time workers plus prorated part-time/freelance work
- **contractor treatment:** Freelancers prorated into FTEs. Not a full-time employee-only count.
- **nationality definition:** Not reported
- **method:** GIC/TIGA company database with company-by-company verification, surveys and desk estimates for missing firms; each edition maps active businesses.
- **sample size:** 2016: 534 responding or public-data companies, 70% of headcount; later editions active company universe.
- **comparison note:** Survey endpoint snapshots, not annual averages. 2025 exact FTE not disclosed in inspected summary. No 2019 observation; never use 2018 or 2020 as 2019 baseline.

### Annual average filled jobs in computer games (GB_dcms_jobs)

Unit: jobs. Population: national_estimate. Geography: domestic_residence.

- **industry scope:** SIC 58.21 Publishing of Computer Games and 62.011 Ready-made interactive leisure and entertainment software development
- **occupation scope:** All occupations within covered SIC activities
- **geography note:** APS UK household survey; workplace region reported for jobs. Not a census of corporate payroll locations.
- **worker status:** First and second filled jobs, employed and self-employed
- **contractor treatment:** Self-employed jobs included; employment-type subdivisions unpublished in games column.
- **nationality definition:** Not reported
- **method:** ONS Annual Population Survey weighted estimates, calendar-year mean. Source values in thousands, converted to jobs by multiplying by 1,000.
- **sample size:** Small game-sector APS sample; 2024 CV 14%, 2025 CV 11%.
- **comparison note:** Official statistics in development; accreditation suspended August 2025 over APS quality. Plot with uncertainty, not as reliable annual hiring/layoff change; do not combine with TIGA or use in headline index.

### Freelancer share of Ukie workforce respondents (GB_ukie_freelancer_share)

Unit: percent. Population: survey_sample. Geography: domestic_residence.

- **industry scope:** UK games industry: production and non-production business roles
- **occupation scope:** All roles in responding workforce
- **geography note:** People reporting work in UK games industry; self-selected sample, not foreign-owned firms or UK company staff abroad.
- **worker status:** Responding workers, company owners/directors and freelancers
- **contractor treatment:** Freelancer self-identification separate from full-time fixed-term employment.
- **nationality definition:** Self-reported UK/British, EU/EEA or rest-of-world nationality. 2021 allows multiple nationalities; 2025 dual-citizenship handling not explicitly verified.
- **method:** Employer links and open-link recruitment; respondent shares, not population census.
- **sample size:** 2021 n=3603 people; 2025-26 n=1610; question n may vary.
- **comparison note:** Recruitment changes from 36% open link in 2021 to 71% in 2025-26. No national gross-up, no causal interpretation of differences, and no summed foreign-national share where categories can overlap.

## Limits

- TIGA excludes non-production commercial/administrative occupations and is not all game-company employment.
- TIGA full report is paid; only public primary summary and methods inspected.
- 2025 contractor value is a strict lower bound, and no exact 2025 FTE was verified.
- TIGA has no 2019 snapshot; do not fabricate a 2019 index baseline.
- DCMS 2025 estimate is 58,000 jobs with a 95% interval of 46,000-71,000; it is not evidence that TIGA is wrong or that employment recovered. Different scope and sample uncertainty apply.
- Ukie nationality percentages are self-reported sampled citizenship categories, not foreign ownership; 2021 multiple-nationality responses cannot be added into a foreign-only total.
- No annual national contractor or nationality census established.
