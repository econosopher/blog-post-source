# Cyprus

CYGMA reports 1,438 people employed and paying taxes locally in 2019 and 4,320 in 2024. A 2025 report edition does not mean 2025 employment. Administrative coverage is broader than a pure games-publishing activity code.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### People employed and paying taxes in Cyprus (CY_local)

Unit: people. Population: administrative_population. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** Video game development/publishing company mapping; excludes iGaming as an intended scope. Administrative J58/J62 categories are imperfect proxies. Hardware, retail, venues and indirect jobs are not the intended total. Specialist services not separately quantified.
- **occupation scope:** All roles in covered businesses, including creative, technical and business functions.
- **geography note:** Source explicitly describes people employed and paying taxes in Cyprus. Citizenship and ownership not used as exclusions; offshore/remote treatment beyond this condition unknown.
- **worker status:** People employed and paying taxes; employee/founder/contractor/apprentice/intern split unknown.
- **contractor treatment:** Not separately specified; do not add contractor estimates.
- **nationality definition:** Not reported.
- **method:** Company and tax/statistical data assembled by CBusiness Advisors. Report identifies broad NACE classification and omitted-head-office issues.
- **sample size:** See observations.
- **comparison note:** Continuous 2019-2024 chart from one report vintage; domestic tax employment is explicit. Index is a within-series change only. Pure core scope and contractor coverage remain imperfect.

## Limits

- Series comes from company and administrative mapping with imperfect activity-code boundaries.
- Foreign citizenship, work permits, foreign ownership and staff abroad are not interchangeable; none is quantified by the employment chart.
- No verified employment observations for 2015-2018 or 2025 in this source.

## Source checks

- **statistical_agency**: CYSTAT employment enterprise NACE 58.21 games. [Source](https://www.cystat.gov.cy/en/StaticPage?id=1226). General employment tables found; no separately verified comprehensive games workforce. CYGMA combines administrative sources.
- **industry_association**: CYGMA Cyprus video game industry report 2025 employment. [Source](https://cygma.eu/news/cyprus-video-game-industry-report-2025-published/). Full original 2025 report downloaded, release dated 17 August 2026; historical employment inspected.
- **local_language**: Κύπρος απασχόληση έκδοση ηλεκτρονικών παιχνιδιών Στατιστική Υπηρεσία. [Source](https://library.cystat.gov.cy/Documents/Publication/EMPLOYMENT_ENT-NACE2-SIZE-2019-161220.pdf). Greek statistical-agency route located enterprise employment publication; broader classifications cannot substitute for the mapped games series.
