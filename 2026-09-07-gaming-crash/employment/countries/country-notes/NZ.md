# New Zealand

NZGDA annual survey observations span 2015-2025. Source units alternate between full-time employees, workers and FTEs, while respondents change. Later editions mention overseas staff. Keep snapshots and visa composition separate; no national index.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### Survey full-time employees / roles (NZ_fulltime)

Unit: full_time_positions. Population: survey_sample. Geography: mixed.

- **industry scope:** Games and interactive studios; later surveys explicitly include VR/AR, education technology and serious games. Contract development and studio services included; hardware, retail, venues and indirect employment not the intended scope.
- **occupation scope:** All roles in covered businesses, including creative, technical and business functions.
- **geography note:** New Zealand studios, all ownerships; 2017/18 explicitly workers in NZ studios. Later respondents include remote teams in NZ and overseas. 2024 reports 31 FTE Australia and 85 other overseas, without proving if included in headline. No subtraction made.
- **worker status:** Source wording shown per observation. Contractors, founders, agency staff, apprentices and interns not separately defined.
- **contractor treatment:** unknown
- **nationality definition:** Not reported.
- **method:** Annual voluntary association studio survey; sample sizes and source wording vary. No documented national gross-up or full-time-equivalence conversion across all years.
- **sample size:** See observations.
- **comparison note:** Fiscal years ending 31 March; reference-date exceptions in country notes. Variable samples and source units; no line trend or index. Overseas-office inclusion unresolved in later summaries.

## Limits

- Respondent pool varies from 27 studios in 2015 to 100 in 2024; 2025 response count not stated in release.
- 2019 workers are not automatically treated as 2019 FTEs.
- Visa composition has changing definitions and rounded percentages; 2024 contains a 97/98 internal conflict.
- Foreign ownership of studios is distinct from foreign citizenship and work abroad.
- 2015-2025 source-native observations are present; no claim of constant population or scope.
