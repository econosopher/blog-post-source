# South Korea

KOCCA reports 54,285 development/publishing workers in 2024, separately from 33,291 PC-cafe and arcade-venue workers. Verified annual core observations span 2015–2024. Repeated vintages show a one-person 2017 revision, retained explicitly.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### Game development and publishing workers (KR_core)

Unit: people. Population: national_estimate. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** PC, mobile, console and arcade-game production/publishing; excludes PC cafes and arcade venues
- **occupation scope:** All occupations within covered businesses
- **geography note:** People working in the country; nationality not implied.
- **worker status:** Source-reported workers; detailed status treatment not specified
- **contractor treatment:** 2017 status table distinguishes regular, nonregular and agency employees; independent freelancers not expressly measured.
- **nationality definition:** Not reported
- **method:** Annual KOCCA/MCST industry statistical survey; source-reported national headcount estimates.
- **sample size:** unknown
- **comparison note:** Domestic development/publishing. Latest survey frame excludes operating firms with no revenue; when this rule began is unresolved. Index withheld because population-frame continuity is not fully audited.

## Limits

- Full survey population-frame changes were not exhaustively audited. The 2025 summary excludes operating firms with zero revenue as temporarily closed.
- Core includes arcade-game developers. PC cafes/arcade venue operators are only in the separate broad series.
- Nationality and independent freelancer counts were not verified.
- One-person differences across vintages and platform sums are retained, not silently reconciled.
