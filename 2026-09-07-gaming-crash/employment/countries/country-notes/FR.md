# France

EY reports direct employee FTE excluding independent workers: exact endpoints 6,674 in 2019 and 9,944 in 2024. The preferred 2019–2024 annual series uses the consistently rounded employment chart. Scope includes developers, publishers and technology suppliers. SNJV contract shares remain sample-only.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### Direct games-sector employee FTE, excluding independent workers (FR_ey_direct_fte)

Unit: FTE. Population: national_estimate. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** Video-game publishers, developers and technology suppliers; excludes console/accessory manufacturing, local associations and esports organisations. Measured using EY weighted industry-code allocations.
- **occupation scope:** All occupations in covered organisations
- **geography note:** See comparison note; local operations rather than nationality unless explicitly stated
- **worker status:** Employee FTE; independent workers explicitly excluded by the games-sector definition on printedp 54. Salaried owner treatment is not separately described.
- **contractor treatment:** Independent workers excluded. Salaried employment at included technology suppliers remains within the sector; no separate employee-contract breakdown.
- **nationality definition:** all citizenships
- **method:** INSEE ESANE, company accounts in Diane, URSSAF and EY sector allocation
- **sample size:** unknown
- **comparison note:** Domestic direct employee FTE. Games-specificp 54 exclusion of independent workers overrides the generic appendix discussion of non-salaried work. Preferred annual chart retains the consistent roundedp 56 series; exactp 54 endpoints 6674 and 9944 are separately preserved nonpreferred. No splice with older EY editions.

## Limits

- 2015–2018 not recovered with a safely compatible definition.
- Foreign capital 94% French is ownership, not citizenship; no valid nationality share located.
- Independent workers are excluded, so the direct employee FTE total does not capture the entire games labour force. Weighted industry-code method and technology-supplier coverage differ from a developer-only census.
