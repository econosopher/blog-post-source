# Japan

CESA estimates 58,000–83,000 workers in core game companies and about 200,000 including related industries, but the public 2025 release does not date its employment observation. It also includes console hardware in core. CEDEC provides sample-only worker-status composition for 2024 and 2025.

Coverage: usable_snapshot. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Limits

- No continuous domestic developer/publisher series was verified for 2015–latest.
- Employment year for the CESA range remains unknown. Hardware makes the core scope broader than studios alone.
- Full CESA employment estimation methodology is behind a paid publication.
- CEDEC respondent proportions are not national workforce estimates; no nationality share verified.
