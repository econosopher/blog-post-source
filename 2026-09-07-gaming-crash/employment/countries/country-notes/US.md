# United States

Periodic ESA firm-mapping estimates cover 2015, 2016, 2019, 2023 and 2025. Core development/publishing can be derived from explicit subsectors for 2019/2023/2025. Broad totals are not a consistent time series; no verified national contractor or foreign-national share was found.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### Core developer and publisher company employment (US_core)

Unit: jobs. Population: national_estimate. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** Game development and publishing
- **occupation scope:** All roles in covered businesses
- **geography note:** Domestic operations, including foreign-owned companies
- **worker status:** Company workforce; precise employment-status scope varies
- **contractor treatment:** Individuals with contractor status not separately enumerated; specialist service firms excluded from this derived subset.
- **nationality definition:** Not reported
- **method:** Sum of Developer + Developer/Publisher + Publisher categories in ESA firm database.
- **sample size:** Not fixed across vintages
- **comparison note:** Definition close across three editions, but database methodology changed. Plot discrete observations or explicit methodology breaks; no 2019 index.

## Limits

- No complete annual national series: unpublished years remain blank.
- 2015/2016 mapping totals and 2019 broad total cannot be joined as the same scope.
- US geography follows domestic employees, including US workers of foreign firms; not US citizenship.
- 2026 PDF summary graphic/text layers contain inconsistent values; main Table1 gives 63,248 software and 82,930 overall.
- No verified national contractor or nationality counts; unknown is not zero.
