# Netherlands

Games Monitor gives 3, 834 jobs in 2018, 4, 560 in 2021 and 4, 291 in 2023. Most recent decline reflects companies moving outside the games definition. Includes self-employed specialists and applied games.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### Dutch games-industry jobs including self-employed specialists (NL_domestic_broad)

Unit: jobs. Population: administrative_population. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** Development, production, publishing, facilitation and electronic distribution; entertainment and applied games; specialist services and freelancers; excludes retail and gambling
- **occupation scope:** All occupations in covered organisations
- **geography note:** See comparison note; local operations rather than nationality unless explicitly stated
- **worker status:** Employees and active self-employed specialists; founders with main employment elsewhere not active jobs
- **contractor treatment:** Specialist freelancers and one-person subcontractors included; no independent contractor share
- **nationality definition:** all citizenships
- **method:** LISA employment register, survey, company websites and LinkedIn cross-checks
- **sample size:** unknown
- **comparison note:** Same published definition; most 2021–2023 decline is reclassification of company focus, not job loss. No 2019 observation.
