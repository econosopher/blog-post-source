# China

CNG reports a selected major-listed-company personnel panel falling from 223,000 in 2021 to 205,800 in 2024. This is not verified as domestic game-only employment. The widely quoted 2018 national figure has undefined boundaries and cannot be joined to the panel.

Coverage: context_only. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.
