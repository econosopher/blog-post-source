# Spain

Annual 2015–2024 development-sector direct employment. External freelancers, foreign-origin workers and permanent-contract shares are separate; 2025 report was released in 2026 and forecasts are excluded.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### Direct employment in Spanish game development (ES_direct)

Unit: people. Population: national_estimate. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** Active Spanish game-development companies including outsourcing/codesvelopment and other studio activities
- **occupation scope:** All occupations in covered organisations
- **geography note:** See comparison note; local operations rather than nationality unless explicitly stated
- **worker status:** Direct workers; external freelance collaborators reported separately
- **contractor treatment:** External freelancers excluded from direct headline, separately recorded
- **nationality definition:** all citizenships
- **method:** DEV studio survey and estimation
- **sample size:** 2025 report:N=283;estimated universe 500 active companies
- **comparison note:** Domestic development-company workers; precise handling of owners and cross-border direct staff not fully specified. Retrospective direct series; explicit external freelancers separated.

## Limits

- Foreign capital is ownership, not worker citizenship.
- Older reports mix salary workers and collaborators in prose; primary retrospective direct series used.
- 2025–2028 figures next to 2024 are forecasts, excluded.
- Freelance count geography and duplicate engagements unverified.

## Source checks

- **statistical_agency**: site.ine.es videojuegos empleo desarrollo. [Source](https://www.ine.es/). Search returned broader/time-use material rather than dedicated development population; DEV used.
- **industry_association**: DEV Libro Blanco historical 2015 latest employment. [Source](https://dev.org.es/libro_blanco/). Read 2018, 2020, 2023, 2025 primary editions; 2023 retrospective history supplies 2015 onward.
- **local_language**: Libro Blanco 2015 2016 empleo directo extranjeros freelance. [Source](https://dev.org.es/libro-blanco-2020/). Original Spanish wording distinguishes origin from citizenship; external freelancing from direct jobs.
