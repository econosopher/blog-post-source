# Finland

Biennial studio employment 2016–2024; global totals include offices abroad. Domestic 2024 explicitly FTE and includes entrepreneurs. Sample nationality shares kept apart from national estimates.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### Finnish-studio employment including overseas offices (FI_studios_global)

Unit: people. Population: national_estimate. Geography: company_worldwide.

- **industry scope:** Finnish game development studios, including foreign-owned operations in Finland and Finnish-owned offices abroad
- **occupation scope:** All occupations in covered organisations
- **geography note:** See comparison note; local operations rather than nationality unless explicitly stated
- **worker status:** Employees and entrepreneurs
- **contractor treatment:** Entrepreneurs included; external freelancers not separately quantified
- **nationality definition:** all citizenships
- **method:** 71 studio interviews supplemented by public sources; 185 studios with employee data in 2024
- **sample size:** 71 interviews; 185 studios with employee information
- **comparison note:** Publisher labels total people; domestic 2024 component described as FTE. Not country-comparable; no 2019 baseline.

## Limits

- FTE and people terms overlap in publisher wording; separate explicit domestic FTE.
- 2015 and odd years absent from benchmark series.
- Entrepreneurs included; separate external contractor count not found.

## Source checks

- **statistical_agency**: site.stat.fi peliala työntekijät 2024. [Source](https://stat.fi/en/statistics/tyokay). General employment tables found; no clean whole-games national series located.
- **industry_association**: Neogames 2024 report employment nationality. [Source](https://neogames.fi/about-the-industry/). Full report read; 185-company employment evidence vs 71-company interviews separated.
- **local_language**: Neogames 2024 kansalaisuutta kansainvälistä. [Source](https://neogames.fi/fi/kansainvalinen-liiketoiminta-vaatii-kansainvalista-osaamista/). Local-language association commentary defines international talent as people without Finnish citizenship.
