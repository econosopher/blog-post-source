# Canada

Annual Statistics Canada administrative ILUs cover 2015-2022, separate from periodic ESAC FTE estimates. The latest ESAC comparison is 35,250 revised FTEs in 2020-21 versus 34,010 in 2023-24. Contractor shares and 2015 immigration-status shares are survey-based, not comparable national citizenship counts.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### Game design and publishing employment, administrative ILUs (CA_statcan_ilu)

Unit: ILU. Population: administrative_population. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** NAICS 513212 video game publishers and 541515 video game design/development services
- **occupation scope:** All roles in covered businesses
- **geography note:** Canadian provincial firms; territories excluded. Province assigned by firm headquarters, not each worker location.
- **worker status:** T4 wage/salary recipients; each individual totals one ILU across issuing firms in a calendar year.
- **contractor treatment:** Non-payroll independent contractors not counted by the T4 ILU measure; self-employed businesses can enter firm universe but not automatically employment.
- **nationality definition:** Not reported
- **method:** Linked business registry and tax microdata. One ILU per T4 recipient per year; split between firms when multiple T4s, including firms outside games.
- **sample size:** All eligible firms in provincial administrative microdata; annual universe varies.
- **comparison note:** Consistent annual unit, but ILU is neither FTE nor point-in-time headcount. Use Table 3 values: adjacent prose incorrectly describes a 2022 total decline.

### Direct industry FTEs, earlier ESAC method (CA_esac_legacy)

Unit: FTE. Population: national_estimate. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** Companies developing, publishing/selling video games and directly related services
- **occupation scope:** All roles in covered businesses
- **geography note:** Domestic operations, including foreign-owned companies
- **worker status:** Full-time, part-time and contract/freelance workforce expressed in FTEs
- **contractor treatment:** Contract/freelance workers included; no separate national individual headcount.

- **sample size:** Not fixed across vintages

### 2015 surveyed workforce by immigration/citizenship status (CA_immigration_share)

Unit: percent. Population: survey_sample. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** Companies developing, publishing/selling video games and directly related services
- **occupation scope:** All roles in covered businesses
- **geography note:** Domestic operations, including foreign-owned companies
- **worker status:** Current workforce of responding companies
- **contractor treatment:** Treatment by status not separately specified
- **nationality definition:** Temporary foreign worker; permanent resident; naturalized Canadian citizen. Distinct immigration-status categories, not a foreign-citizenship total.
- **method:** Employer-reported current workforce status shares
- **sample size:** 46 responding companies
- **comparison note:** Naturalized citizens are Canadian citizens; never count them as foreign nationals. Permanent residence and TFW status do not equal place of birth. No national gross-up.

## Limits

- FTE, ILU and people are not interchangeable. ESAC and StatCan cover different firm universes.
- ESAC survey years refer to mixed prior-fiscal-year/current-business data; preserve 2020-21 and 2023-24 labels.
- 2015 FTE reference year ambiguous; omitted from calendar-year chart.
- 2021 FTE revision prevents an unbroken ESAC series through 2019 without a marked methodological break.
- StatCan Table 3 grows to 59,689 in 2022; contradictory nearby prose cannot support a decline claim.
- ESAC foreign-owned employment share is company ownership, not individual nationality.
- No newer ESAC national employment observation than 2023-24 or newer StatCan annual observation than 2022 was verified by cutoff.
