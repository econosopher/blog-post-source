# Australia

Two distinct measures are available: ABS domestic business employment (headcount) and IGEA full-time employment including FTEs and contractors. IGEA added nonresponding-studio estimates for FY2025; do not interpret that change as a consistent survey trend.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### IGEA full-time employment incl. contractors (AU_igea_survey)

Unit: source_reported_full_time_employment. Population: survey_sample. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** Australian game development studios including contracted development and all internal roles; broader publishing distribution, hardware, retail, venues, esports and indirect employment not included as separate sectors.
- **occupation scope:** All roles in covered businesses, including creative, technical and business functions.
- **geography note:** Domestic workers irrespective of citizenship or employer ownership are the comparison target; exact source rules below.
- **worker status:** Source says full-time employees, includes full-time equivalents and contractors. Conversion of every contractor to FTE is not established. Founders/interns/apprentices not separately specified.
- **contractor treatment:** Included in source total; no separate count. Do not label as pure employee headcount or pure FTE.
- **nationality definition:** Not reported.
- **method:** Voluntary studio survey, changing respondent pool; no verified full-population expansion for historical series.
- **sample size:** See observations.
- **comparison note:** Financial years ending 30 June. Changing studio samples; no continuous population estimate or index.

### IGEA survey plus studio estimates (AU_igea_augmented)

Unit: source_reported_full_time_employment. Population: national_estimate. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** Australian game development studios including contracted development and all internal roles; broader publishing distribution, hardware, retail, venues, esports and indirect employment not included as separate sectors.
- **occupation scope:** All roles in covered businesses, including creative, technical and business functions.
- **geography note:** Domestic workers irrespective of citizenship or employer ownership are the comparison target; exact source rules below.
- **worker status:** Full-time employees including FTEs and contractors; other statuses unknown.
- **contractor treatment:** Included in total per p1 footnote; no separate count or full conversion specification.
- **nationality definition:** Not reported.
- **method:** FY2025 voluntary responses from 141 studios plus third-party estimates for selected nonrespondents (FAQ). Not a demonstrated exhaustive national census.
- **sample size:** 141 usable studio responses; over 600 invited; supplementary estimates for selected nonrespondents.
- **comparison note:** Methodology break from historical survey. Source_native total retained; do not calculate a consistent 2024-25 decline.

### ABS people employed in development businesses (AU_abs_headcount)

Unit: people. Population: national_estimate. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** Digital game development businesses, own-title and fee-for-service production; all internal roles. Broader hardware, retail, venues and indirect jobs excluded. Other activities within multi-activity business units may remain.
- **occupation scope:** All roles in covered businesses, including creative, technical and business functions.
- **geography note:** Domestic workers irrespective of citizenship or employer ownership are the comparison target; exact source rules below.
- **worker status:** Working proprietors and partners, payroll employees including paid-leave and workers compensation, and contract workers paid through payroll included. Non-salaried directors and volunteers excluded. Apprentices and interns not separately quantified.
- **contractor treatment:** Payroll contract workers included. Payments to self-employed contractors are outside wages and salaries; off-payroll contractor headcount not included as an employee count.
- **nationality definition:** Not reported.
- **method:** Census of known digital game development businesses; ABS Business Register plus industry sources. Last pay period ending June. Scope/classification changes require caution across survey years.
- **sample size:** Census of known in-scope businesses; response count not extracted.
- **comparison note:** Headcount snapshots, no 2019 baseline. ABS cautions comparisons to 2015-16 as categories changed; do not join to IGEA.

## Limits

- Source-native full-time employment measure must not be renamed pure FTE.
- IGEA FY2025 supplementary employment estimates alter coverage; 2024 and 2025 not used for measured decline.
- 2015 and 2018 IGEA employment not located; ABS series is sparse. No verified nationality breakdown.
