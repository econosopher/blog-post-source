# Poland

Game Industry Conference/PARP reports domestic game-production workforce snapshots of 12, 110 in 2021, 15, 290 in 2023 and 14, 568 in 2025, plus foreign-worker figures.2025 studio coverage changed; auxiliary services excluded.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### Domestic game-production workforce (PL_core_workforce)

Unit: people. Population: national_estimate. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** Actual game production including external development; excludes auxiliary service-only player support/localisation
- **occupation scope:** All occupations in covered organisations
- **geography note:** See comparison note; local operations rather than nationality unless explicitly stated
- **worker status:** Game-production people in developers, publishers and external development studios; individual B 2 B/owner split unknown
- **contractor treatment:** Outsourcing development-company staff included; B 2 B contract share not quantified
- **nationality definition:** all citizenships
- **method:** GIC compiled industry data; PARP questionnaires and company/public-source verification
- **sample size:** unknown
- **comparison note:** Studio counting changed in 2025; the publisher says this is the only methodological shift. Workforce data can also be corrected as source coverage improves. Chart is an estimated industry workforce series, not a payroll census.

## Limits

- 2015–2019 not established as validated annual series; no back-calculation from growth rates.
- 2025 active-studio discovery change documented; no automatic 2019 index.
- Foreign count definition is not proven identical to citizenship.
- Publisher uses cautious estimation and interpolation for missing underlying source data. No annual interpolation has been performed in this extraction; only published snapshot estimates are recorded.
