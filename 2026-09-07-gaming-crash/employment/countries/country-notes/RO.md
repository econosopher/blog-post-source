# Romania

RGDA snapshots show 6, 800 employees/collaborators in 2022, 6, 600 in 2023 and 6, 100 employees in 2024. Wording changed in 2025 release, so employee-only and collaborator-inclusive populations remain separate.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.
