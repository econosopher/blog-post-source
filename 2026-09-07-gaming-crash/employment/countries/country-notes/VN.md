# Vietnam

A government news article repeats Vietnam Briefing’s 4,100 workers in 2024, but the underlying article also claims 35,000 developers without reconciling scopes. A 2025 Nhan Dan interview estimates nearly 10,000 people specifically in mobile games. These are conflicting or differently bounded contextual estimates, not a comparable annual national core series.

Coverage: context_only. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.
