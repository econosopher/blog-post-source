# Taiwan

TAICCA reports 9,929 employees among 133 responding companies in its 2022 games survey, using public job-board data to fill some missing answers. This is a respondent-company sum with a broad value-chain scope, not a national workforce estimate. The 2023 and 2024 report landing pages were located, but their newer employment tables were not retrieved.

Coverage: context_only. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.
