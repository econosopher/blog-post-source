# India

Government-hosted EY report estimates around 66,000 gaming professionals, while a 2025 Primus report says 130,000 in more than 1,900 gaming companies. Their observation dates and gaming/RMG boundaries are not sufficiently defined for a national core comparison; no synthetic trend is created.

Coverage: context_only. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Limits

- India reporting often combines video games, online real-money gaming, esports and wider AVGC.
- 66,000 and 130,000 estimates conflict; dates and worker definitions do not allow reconciliation.
- Forecasts of 250,000 jobs and association vision of 500,000+ jobs are not employment observations.
- No verified national contractor or foreign-national proportion.
