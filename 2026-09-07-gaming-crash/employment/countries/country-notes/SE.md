# Sweden

Annual 2015–2024 domestic annual-average full-time positions, with payroll/owner and consultant exclusions. Some late accounts and retrospective cohort exclusions limit literal year-end comparisons.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### Domestic annual-average full-time positions (SE_domestic)

Unit: annual_average_full_time_positions. Population: administrative_population. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** Developers, publishers and game-specialist subcontracting companies, primarily limited companies
- **occupation scope:** All occupations in covered organisations
- **geography note:** See comparison note; local operations rather than nationality unless explicitly stated
- **worker status:** Salaried employees and owners receiving salary or benefits; unpaid owners/dividend-only owners excluded
- **contractor treatment:** Game-specialist subcontractor company employment covered; consultants in outside-industry firms and interns excluded
- **nationality definition:** all citizenships
- **method:** Audited annual reports, business registers and manual industry selection
- **sample size:** unknown
- **comparison note:** Domestic series, distinct from overseas subsidiaries. 2024 sample excludes approximately 40 positions at firms bankrupt in 2025. King uses 2023 accounts. No conversion to year-end people.
