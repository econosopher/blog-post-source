# Turkey

TOGED supplies a 2023 employment estimate of 10,777 in the EGDF report. The questionnaire requests FTEs including remote workers abroad, so this is not strictly domestic workplace employment. A much lower 2,500-3,000 secondary estimate cannot be verified in its cited original and remains unplotted context.

Coverage: usable_snapshot. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### TOGED developer/publisher workforce estimate (TR_toged)

Unit: FTE. Population: national_estimate. Geography: mixed.

- **industry scope:** Game development and publishing; excludes non-development specialist services, pure asset suppliers, retail and distribution in EGDF questionnaire. Hardware, venues, gambling and esports boundaries not individually specified for Turkey.
- **occupation scope:** All roles in covered businesses, including creative, technical and business functions.
- **geography note:** Includes direct remote employees in third countries under EGDF rules. Excludes staff of foreign subsidiaries and subcontractor companies abroad. Foreign-owned local subsidiaries are reported independently; nationality is not an exclusion.
- **worker status:** EGDF FTE definition includes employees, entrepreneurs and in-house freelancers. Agency staff, apprentices and interns not separately specified; Turkey-specific implementation not documented.
- **contractor treatment:** In-house freelancers in requested FTE measure; foreign subcontractor companies excluded. No separate contractor count.
- **nationality definition:** Not reported.
- **method:** TOGED national submission to EGDF. Questionnaire requests full-time equivalent employees, entrepreneurs and in-house freelancers; country-specific sampling details not published.
- **sample size:** See observations.
- **comparison note:** Usable association snapshot only; mixed remote geography and missing local method prevent strict domestic comparison or a trend. No 2019 observation.

## Limits

- No verified employment observations for 2015-2022 or later than 2023; later market reports do not establish later workforce counts.
- EGDF requests FTEs but the country chart says people; unit implementation and national estimation method are not exposed.
- Remote workers abroad remain within TOGED/EGDF measure; it is not a residents-only or workplace-only total.
- Secondary estimate 2500-3000 is unresolved, not an alternative preferred national total.
