Domestic game-production payroll FTE is verified for 2017–2022 in the 2024 edition. Earlier 2015–2017 and 2016–2019 vintages are retained separately because the population was redefined. The June 2026 Tableau workbook was downloaded and decoded: it covers film, TV and advertising only, with no games segment.

Coverage: usable_series. Research cutoff: 9 September 2026.

## Definitions

### Domestic game-production payroll FTE (DK_payroll_fte)

Unit: FTE. Population: administrative_population. Geography: domestic_workplace.

- **industry scope:** Core game content producers; excludes serious games/XR classified under Interactive
- **occupation scope:** All occupations in covered organisations
- **geography note:** See comparison note; local operations rather than nationality unless explicitly stated
- **worker status:** Payroll employees, including part-time hours converted by publisher; excludes invoice-only work and sole-proprietor hours
- **contractor treatment:** Payroll-covered temporary/freelance employment counts as hours; invoice contractors excluded
- **nationality definition:** all citizenships
- **method:** Statistics Denmark registry linked to manually selected producer company population
- **sample size:** unknown
- **comparison note:** 2024 edition reconstructs five previous years. Company population revised in 2021; use within-edition history, not naive edition splicing.

## Limits

- No nationality share found.
- Foreign subsidiaries excluded; freelancers paid through invoices excluded.
- Latest verified games observation remains 2022. The newer June 2026 public Tableau release covers only film, TV and advertising; all three extracts were checked for every segment.
- 2015 has only the pre 2019 scope;2016 is available under the 2021 cohort. They are separate source vintages, not a continuous 2015–2022 growth series.
