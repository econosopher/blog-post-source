# Country findings at the publication checkpoint

## Turkey

The new EGDF/VGE report's Turkey panel (p 58) gives **10,777 in 2023 and 14,918 in 2024**, attributed to TOGED. The main reviewer checked the rendered panel and questionnaire (p 78). The questionnaire is FTE-oriented and assigns remote workers abroad to the employer-registration country. Turkey-specific sampling and estimation details remain unresolved.

The TOBB/TOGED conference report's **25,000** claim has no established workforce reference date or counting method. It is context, not a 2026 time-series observation. The conference date must not be substituted for the workforce date.

A 2017 academic article repeats an earlier 2000-worker estimate attributed toEGDF, but its underlying workforce year and original evidence remain unresolved. An earlier 5000-worker map claim is likewise not a verified dated observation. Neither is in this checkpoint CSV. Turkey has only two verified dated points, so remains excluded under the current chart's minimum-three-point display rule.

## United States

The historical ESA/Siwek series, ESA Mapping Project, and later TEConomy sector counts have different universes. Keep them separate. TableD 1 in the 2014 report labels 42527 as 2011; TableD 2 labels the all-establishment 42975 as 2012. The published components contain a one-person discrepancy; preserve reported totals and record the issue rather than silently correcting them.

The 2016 mapping figure has a wider reporting universe than the 2013-2015 comparison. The later broad five-sector figures include retail and hardware-related employment. Software-sector values overlap those totals. Government software-publisher codes include non-game businesses and are not a game-only substitute.

The 2026 report says database coverage improved between 2023 and 2025, including more small/solo studios and remote workers. These are not fixed-panel observations. Do not infer a causal layoff decomposition from the totals alone.

## Japan

The prior research recovered 2012 and 2016 Economic Census observations for JSIC 3914 game-software establishments. CESA ecosystem estimates, CEDEC survey respondent counts and METI enterprise-survey division counts have different scopes. The Gemini run did not extend verified time coverage. Full country records remain in the local archive pending import; Japan observations are not reconstructed here.

## Finland

Neogames reports an industry study almost every other year since 2004. The historical Finnish-studio series includes overseas staff and changing worker definitions; it belongs in a separately qualified historical view. The domestic 2022/2024 FTE pair is not interchangeable with those headline totals. The full historical companion and its evidence await archive import.
